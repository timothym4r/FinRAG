Item 1A. Risk Factors
Our operating results may fluctuate based on vehicle demand, pricing actions, battery supply, manufacturing ramp execution, and the pace of energy deployment. Concentration in key facilities, suppliers, and strategic programs could amplify operational volatility.

Management's Discussion and Analysis
Revenue trends reflected vehicle pricing dynamics, production efficiencies, energy storage growth, and continued investment in autonomy, AI infrastructure, and manufacturing scale. Margin performance was affected by mix, factory utilization, and cost-down execution.

Financial Statements
The consolidated statements capture the combined effects of automotive deliveries, energy generation and storage, and service operations.

Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements
We monitor inventory, warranties, leasing residual values, commodity exposure, and supplier concentration as part of our risk and capital planning framework.
