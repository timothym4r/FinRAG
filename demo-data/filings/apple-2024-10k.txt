Item 1A. Risk Factors
Our business depends on concentrated manufacturing, logistics, and supplier relationships that could introduce timing, cost, and inventory risk if disrupted. Demand can also be affected by macroeconomic conditions, foreign exchange volatility, regulatory actions, or shifts in consumer spending.

Management's Discussion and Analysis
Net sales performance reflected continued services momentum, product mix shifts across iPhone, Mac, and wearables, and ongoing investment in silicon, retail, and platform capabilities. Gross margin trends were influenced by component costs, mix, and foreign currency impacts.

Financial Statements
Consolidated statements reflect the scale of our product and services ecosystem, cash generation profile, and capital return program.

Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements
We continue to evaluate supply commitments, geographic exposure, tax matters, and concentration effects tied to manufacturing and channel execution.
